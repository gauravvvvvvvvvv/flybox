## MaleCNS data

FLYBOX/FlyBrain uses data derived from the Janelia/FlyEM whole adult male *Drosophila melanogaster* central nervous system connectome ("MaleCNS").

MaleCNS-derived data are made available by their original publishers under their own terms. Public MaleCNS releases are distributed under **CC BY 4.0**; users of those data are responsible for preserving the required attribution.

Project resources:

- https://male-cns.janelia.org/
- https://github.com/flyconnectome/2025malecns

## FlyBrain package

FLYBOX depends on the third-party `flybrain` Python package. The upstream FlyBrain software is distributed under the MIT License.

FLYBOX does not claim ownership of FlyBrain or upstream MaleCNS scientific data.

## Contributions containing data

Do not commit third-party scientific datasets, neuron meshes, skeleton archives, images, or other research assets unless their license permits redistribution and the source/license is documented.

If you add a new dataset or derived scientific asset, update this file and `THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md`.
